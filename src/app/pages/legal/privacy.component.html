02What we collect

There are exactly two ways we receive personal data here.

Information you give us deliberately

When you submit the contact form, we receive:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your company or organisation, if you choose to provide it
  • The subject category you select
  • The content of your message

If you email or telephone us directly, we hold whatever you send in that correspondence. If you apply for a role, we hold your application and anything you link to from it.

Technical information handled by our hosting provider

Serving a web page necessarily involves your IP address, browser user-agent and the URL requested. Our hosting provider processes these to deliver the site and to protect it from abuse. We do not build profiles from this data, and we do not combine it with anything from the contact form.

03Why we collect it

  • To reply to you. This is the only reason we ask for your details on the contact form.
  • To carry out work you have engaged us for, including correspondence about a project.
  • To assess a job application you have sent us.
  • To keep the site available and secure, and to meet legal or accounting obligations where they apply.

We do not use your details for marketing. We do not have a newsletter. Submitting the contact form does not subscribe you to anything, and we will not add you to a list.

04Third parties

We keep this list as short as we can, and it is complete as at the date above.

  • Our email delivery provider transmits contact form submissions to our mailbox. Your name, email address and message pass through their infrastructure in order to be delivered.
  • Our hosting provider serves this website and runs the function that handles the contact form.
  • Google Fonts. The typefaces on this site are loaded from Google's font service, which means your browser makes a request to Google and Google therefore receives your IP address. We mention this because it is a genuine third-party disclosure, even though we gain nothing from it and set no cookie through it.
  • Our email and document provider stores the correspondence once it reaches us, as it would for any business mailbox.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it for advertising. We do not transfer it to anyone else except where we are legally compelled to, or where it is necessary to deliver work you have asked us to do — and in that case we will tell you.

05Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies. It runs no analytics, no heatmaps, no session recording, no advertising pixels and no social tracking scripts. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Should that change — if we add privacy-respecting analytics, for example — we will update this section before deploying it, and add a consent mechanism where one is required.

06How long we keep it

  • Contact form enquiries: up to 24 months from our last exchange, then deleted. Earlier on request.
  • Job applications: up to 12 months, so we can come back to you if a suitable role opens. Earlier on request.
  • Client correspondence and records: for the duration of the engagement and then as long as required for statutory, tax and audit purposes under Indian law.
  • Hosting logs: retained by our provider on their own short rolling schedule, typically days to weeks.

07How we protect it

Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Access to our mailboxes and infrastructure requires multi-factor authentication and is limited to the people who need it for the work. Credentials are held in a password manager, never in code or in a repository. We review access when someone's role changes.

No system is perfect. If a breach occurs that is likely to affect you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as required under the DPDP Act, without undue delay.

08Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to:

  • Confirm and access the personal data we hold about you, and tell you who we have shared it with
  • Correct anything inaccurate, incomplete or out of date
  • Erase it, where we have no lawful reason to keep it
  • Withdraw consent you previously gave, which we will act on going forward
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity
  • Raise a grievance about how we have handled your data

Email {{ emails.legal }} and we will respond within thirty days. We will not charge you for this, and we will not make you justify the request. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you may escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

09Client project data

When we build or operate a system for a client, we often process personal data belonging to that client's own users. In that relationship the client is the data fiduciary and we are a processor acting on their documented instructions.

In practice, that means:

  • Infrastructure, databases and model provider accounts are set up in the client's name, not ours
  • We access production data only where necessary for the agreed work, and prefer anonymised or synthetic data for development
  • Our obligations are set out in the engagement agreement, which takes precedence over this policy for that data
  • On termination we remove our access rather than retaining a copy

If you are an end user of a system we built for someone else, that organisation's privacy policy governs your data — not this one.

10Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. Where a product of ours is available to users under 18, we obtain verifiable parental consent as required by the DPDP Act and do not use their data for tracking or targeted advertising. If you believe a child has submitted data to us, write to {{ emails.legal }} and we will delete it.
